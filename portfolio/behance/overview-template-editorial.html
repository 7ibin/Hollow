Context

Why this project exists

Aggregators teach users to expect speed, but health quotes still mean member lists, disclosures, and dense compare tables. Hollow prototypes how those steps can stay honest and legible on a 390px canvas.

Role

End-to-end product UX

Information architecture, interaction patterns, visual system, and exported hi-fi screens for portfolio and usability walkthroughs.

Constraints

Real-device framing

Static HTML prototype with representative data—designed for capture, critique, and Figma alignment, not live pricing APIs.

Deliverables

What you'll see in this case study

Quote wizards, plan compare, checkout, sign-in, and post-purchase tools—shown as styled Behance slides matching the project cover.
