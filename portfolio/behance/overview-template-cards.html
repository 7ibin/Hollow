Hollow

UI / UX case study · Overview

Mobile insurance has to explain complexity fast—without turning every quote into a form marathon.

01

Where friction shows up

Shoppers switch between individual and family floater, answer health questions per person, then stack three plans in a table built for desktop. On 390px, half-read rows and buried differences feel like hidden risk.

02

What I optimised for

Clear member rules up front, compare that highlights gaps—not noise—and journeys that still work after purchase: policies, claims, and plain-language help in the same shell.

03

What Hollow delivers

A cohesive mobile prototype across health, motor, term, and home—shared wizards, sticky CTAs, AI and expert touchpoints, and a Hollow token system tuned for real device widths.
